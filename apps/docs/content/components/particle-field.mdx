## Usage

```tsx
import { ParticleField } from '@brand-studio/ui';
```

```tsx
<ParticleField count={80}>
  <h2>Shoot by starlight.</h2>
</ParticleField>
```

Specks take the ink colour, and one in five the accent. Keep `count` under about 150 on a large section.

## Accessibility

- The specks are drawn on a canvas hidden from screen readers; your content sits above it.
- It pauses off screen, and reduced motion holds one still frame.
