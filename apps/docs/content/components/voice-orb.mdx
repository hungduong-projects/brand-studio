## Usage

```tsx
import { VoiceOrb } from '@brand-studio/ui';
```

```tsx
<VoiceOrb state="listening" level={level} size={140} />
<VoiceOrb state="thinking" shape="network" />
```

`level` runs from 0 to 1. Feed it the microphone's loudness while listening (DictationButton's `onLevel` gives you this) or the agent's voice while speaking. Idle shows a ring that breathes, listening sends a wave through rows of dots, thinking runs dots round tilted orbits and speaking spreads ripples from the orb's face. Pass `shape` to swap in another look for every state: `network` joins dots with lines, `meridians` weaves dots along vertical lines, `ribbon` rolls a band of strokes round the sphere and `morph` re-forms a ring of dots into a circle, a triangle and a square. A shape runs at half speed while idle and swells with `level`. The dots take the ink colour, so the orb follows light and dark themes. Below 64 px it draws fewer, larger dots.

## Accessibility

- The orb is a status that says its state in words: Ready, Listening, Thinking or Speaking. `label` shows your own words instead.
- The drawing is hidden from screen readers.
- With reduced motion the orb holds still but still follows the level. It also stops drawing when it scrolls off screen.
