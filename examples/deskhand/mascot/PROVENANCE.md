# Deskhand mascot provenance

Deskhand is a fictional product. The mascot is original Brand Studio work drawn for this example.

- `parts/*.svg`: hand-written SVG parts, filled shapes only, in Deskhand's contract colours (`apps/showcase/src/deskhand.brand.json`). The body comes from the Deskhand mark.
- `mascot.json`: the rig, its states and behaviour rules.
- `out/`: rendered with `plugins/brand-studio/skills/brand-design/scripts/mascot.mjs`. Source type `code` (IPTC `algorithmicMedia`).
