# Deskhand figure provenance

Deskhand is a fictional product. The figure is original Brand Studio work drawn for this example.

- `figure.json`: the parts list and steps, written by hand. The desk, its drawers, tray, track and gate are invented for this figure.
- Readouts: ticket #2291, order #48213, the three sources, the $84.00 refund and the $50 limit come from `examples/deskhand/slides/deck.json`.
- Colours: mixed from Deskhand's contract tokens (`apps/showcase/src/deskhand.brand.json`). The accent marks only sources, citations and the approve button, as the contract requires.
- `out/`: exported with `plugins/brand-studio/skills/brand-design/scripts/figure.mjs`; `out/3d/` holds the same parts rendered through three.js. Source type `code` (IPTC `algorithmicMedia`).
