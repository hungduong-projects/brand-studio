# Deskhand slides provenance

Deskhand is a fictional product. The deck's facts come from its brand contract, `plugins/brand-studio/skills/brand-design/assets/deskhand.brand.json`, and its demo page in `apps/showcase`.

- `deck.json`: original outline written for this example.
- `deck.pptx`: built from `deck.json` with `plugins/brand-studio/skills/brand-design/scripts/slides.mjs`.
- `images/console.png`: capture of the Deskhand demo page console (Brand Studio showcase, reduced motion, final state), cropped to the open ticket, 25 Sep 2026. Original Brand Studio work.
- `deck.pdf` and `contact-sheet.png`: LibreOffice render of `deck.pptx` with Geist and Geist Mono (SIL Open Font License) available to the renderer.
